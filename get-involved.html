Get involved

Help us open the door

Six beds in Dallas for people who need transitional housing — a home, not just a roof. However you give, it goes toward a front-door key.

Donate

Our community campaign: $160,000

Our campaign goal buys, renovates, furnishes, and launches the first six-bed home in Dallas — everything between an empty house and a front-door key, including insurance, licensing, and the first weeks of operation.

Once a home is open, government housing programs help cover its ongoing costs, so future residents don’t depend on us re-raising your gift. With stays averaging about six months, one home can welcome more than 90 people over ten years.

Give on GoFundMe Sponsor a bed

The “more than 90 people” estimate assumes six beds, average stays of about six months, and a conservative 75% occupancy.

What your gift does

Every amount has a face

Each level maps to something real: a bed, a room, a move-in day.

$25

Toward a move-in kit: sheets, towels, and toiletries for a resident’s first night

$100

Pantry staples and household supplies for the home for two weeks

$500

A new mattress, frame, and bedding for one resident

$1,200

A shared bedroom fully furnished: two beds, dressers, a desk, and a lamp

$8,000

The entire home furnished — every bedroom, the kitchen, the living room

$25,000

The complete safety renovation: egress, smoke systems, bathrooms, and kitchen

Ways to give

Whatever you have is enough

Sponsor a bed

$100 a month covers one resident’s bed — a person, not a percentage — with recognition in the home.

Give once or monthly

Any amount, on our campaign page. Monthly gifts give the home steady support.

Business matching

Match campaign gifts up to a cap and double every donation while the match lasts, with your logo on our donor wall.

Give things, not money

Furnish a room

Furniture and household goods furnish rooms at almost no cost. Food and pantry items keep house pantries stocked, lower residents’ out-of-pocket costs, and support community meals.

We’ll match what you have to what each home needs right now, and arrange pickup or a drop-off time — so nothing goes to waste.

Offer an item

  • Non-perishable groceries and pantry staples
  • Hygiene products and cleaning supplies

Partner with us

Every organization has a lane

Congregations & faith communities

We welcome churches, mosques, synagogues, temples, and faith-based nonprofits. Invite us for a 10-minute presentation, make a monthly commitment of any size, run food and goods drives, or offer volunteers and mentors. Ten congregations giving $500 a month would provide $60,000 a year of steady support.

Local charities & housing providers

Organizations already doing this work are partners, not competitors. Partners keep their name, board, donors, and mission, and can join our referral network so residents move between providers without starting over.

Agencies & service providers

We want to work alongside the VA, the local Continuum of Care, domestic violence shelters, reentry coalitions, hospital discharge planners, and 211 — so the people you serve can find a home that fits their program.

Businesses

Match campaign gifts, sponsor a bed or a renovation, or support employment programs for residents.
